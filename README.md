# Will the Customer Accept the Coupon?

### Overview

This report analyzes customer coupon acceptance based on factors like weather conditions, demographics, traffic etc. across multiple coupon categories. Its main objective is to find the highest acceptance rate and highlight the factors that contributed to it.

### Key Findings

#### Overall Acceptance Rates
- **Overall coupon acceptance rate**: 56.76% across all coupon types
- **Bar coupons**: 41.00% acceptance rate
- **Coffee house coupons**: 49.86% acceptance rate

The variation in acceptance rates across coupon categories demonstrates external factors significantly influences customer decisions.

#### Demographic Differences

**Age and Venue Frequency:**
- Customers aged 26-30 and over who frequent bars more than once monthly show markedly higher acceptance rates (69.52%) compared to customers aged 25 or younger who visit bars infrequently
- Coffee house coupons show higher acceptance among frequent visitors over 25 years old (63.81%) versus infrequent younger visitors
- This suggests mature, habitual customers are more likely to accept coupons for venues they have been visting regularly

**Income Levels:**
- Lower-income customers (under $50K annually) who frequently dine at inexpensive restaurants ($20 or less) show notable coupon acceptance rates (45.35%)
- This shows price-sensitivity and making them more open to promotional offers

**Passenger Occupation:**
- Customers who are not a kid and in non-farming/fishing/forestry occupations show significantly higher bar coupon acceptance (71.32%) compared to those who are kids and in farming/fishing/forestry occupations
- This indicates that occupational lifestyle influence coupon utilization

**Marital and Family Status:**
- Unmarried drivers or those traveling with partners (excluding those who are widowed) show stronger acceptance rates
- The presence of children substantially reduces bar coupon acceptance

#### Behavioral Patterns

**Venue Frequency is the Strongest Predictor:**
- Customers who visit bars 3+ times monthly have a 76.88% acceptance rate
- Those visiting 3 or fewer times monthly have only 37.05% acceptance
- This 39.83-percentage-point difference is the largest gap observed in the analysis
- Implication: Existing customers are significantly more receptive to promotional offers

**Spending Patterns:**
- Customers who frequently eat at inexpensive restaurants (4+ times monthly) accept coupons at higher rates when combined with lower income levels
- This suggests cross-promotional opportunities between different venue types

### Customer Profiles

**High Acceptance Profile:**
- Frequent visitor to the venue type (4+ times monthly)
- Age 26 or older
- Income variable but responsive to price-conscious messaging
- Travels with partner or alone (without children)
- Non-agricultural occupation

**Low Acceptance Profile:**
- Infrequent visitor (0-1 times monthly)
- Age 25 or younger
- Travels with children or dependents
- Agricultural/forestry occupation

### Conclusion

Coupon acceptance is strongly correlated with **visit frequency**, **age demographics**, **family/social context**, and **income levels**. The most effective coupon campaigns can target existing customers in their preferred venue category, account for their age and family responsibilities, and match the affordability of their income. Venue frequency and familarity looks like the strongest predictor of acceptance, indicating that coupon programs can prioritize reaching out to customers who already have an established relationship with the existing venues.
